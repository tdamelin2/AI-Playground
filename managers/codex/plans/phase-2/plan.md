### State and interface contract
- `world/` owns plain serializable state and ecology; `rules/` owns legal mutation, effects, X and perception; `minds/` holds only no-network dummy/scripted policies and knowledge data in this phase. `Simulation` coordinates; observer receives copies. Avoid world importing a rules module that imports World at module load; use TYPE_CHECKING/local imports where necessary.
- Actor IDs are stable and distinct from database row IDs: players `player_1`..`player_4`, villagers from one persisted monotonic allocator. Human-facing output uses neutral names. Every player has the same initial needs/equipment/task definition, one rotated home anchor and three villagers. Propose 50 total initial villagers: 12 attached and 38 wanderers; no spawning/birth/refill path. Death leaves a permanent dead record; recruitment and rewards transfer a live wanderer rather than creating one.
- Persist Player `{id,name,home,position,mover,body,inventory,adaptation_B,x,feeling,relationships,current_action,task_state,known_places,knowledge,witnesses,pending_interrupts,last_decision_tick}`; Villager `{id,name,leader_id|null,alive,body,loyalty,bonds_by_player,job,work_level,inventory,mover,witnesses}`. Keep bond and loyalty as different numbers and update causes.
- Persist item lots `{id,kind,quantity,created_tick,spoil_due,owner/container}` and tool instances `{id,kind,grade,durability}`. One location per item: actor, structure or tile. `transfer_items` validates exact quantities and commits once; no duplicated inventory on trade, theft, dropped items or death. Food age survives all transfers. Tamed animal `{id,owner,pen_id,feed_due}` is removed from wild wandering/breeding before becoming domestic.
- Persist structures `{id,kind,pos,owner,health,build_progress,contributors,inventory,fire_id}`, fires `{kind,pos,fuel_remaining,house_id}`, projects, alliances, offers, promises, stakes, land owners, task/reward receipts and landmark week counters. Fields and bridges overlay terrain; a bridge changes effective path passability without inventing an unregistered terrain key. `zone_grid` continues describing ecology; land ownership is separate. Claims consume eligible tiles in the existing unclaimed pool; pulling stakes reverses claims according to one documented rule, never creates extra land.
- `ActionRequest` is a strict Pydantic discriminated union with kind-specific target/item/destination fields and positive `amount` or absolute `until_tick`/resolved endpoint where applicable. Reject contradictory endpoints, invalid targets and unknown fields. Internal item names may reveal poison to the engine; sense-facing names must describe appearance, not reveal toxicity before discovery.
- `ActiveAction` persists `{id,actor_id,request,start_tick,work_done,next_unit_due,route,route_index,status}` plus explicit reserved/consumed inputs and contributors. `ActionSpec` binds schema, preconditions, duration/rate keys, work-unit effect and completion effect. An amount is completed output, not repeated rewards on every minute. Side effects occur only when work units complete, including partial project contributions; interruption preserves committed units, fractional effort and project work, never refunds consumed inputs or grants unfinished output. Keep paused action records addressable by ID for explicit resumption; consume a discrete recipe only on successful atomic completion, and return unused reservations on cancellation.
- Proposed callable seam: `validate_action(world, actor_id, request) -> validated request | reason`; `progress_actions(world,tick) -> list[Intent]`; `resolve_intents(world,intents,tick) -> list[Outcome]`; `discover(world,player_id,tick) -> SenseReport`; `controller.decide(report, decision_state) -> ActionRequest`. `decision_state` is a sanitized projection of own allowed inventory/tasks/knowledge, never the full Player or World: exclude even own exact X, raw poison identifiers and unlearned hidden rules. Controllers must not receive another player's private state. Scripted fixtures may arrange setup before the run, then use the same public action path and normal timing.
- One recurring rules job advances every minute even when `Simulation.run()` batches a day. Order: due ecology and animal movement; commit due human route steps and update bodies/fire/spoilage/jobs/action progress; build fresh sense reports and decision requests; collect and validate all decisions against the same view; resolve the complete tick intent set; resolve chat; queue perceived outcomes/interrupts for the next decision; finalize tasks/rewards/metrics and summary. Progress stages candidate effects without prematurely applying competing harvests. An action whose completion commits during resolution raises completion for the next decision phase, when its actual output is visible; never choose a new action on presumed winning output. Freeze exact scheduler orders once: do not execute today's ecology twice or let a new action complete at its start tick. Summaries must run after final rules effects at the day boundary.
- Use rotating stable player order (`tick % 4` offset) for deterministic traversal, and a separate named `actions.conflicts` stream to choose among competing claims on each scarce resource. One winner for the last fish; losing intent cannot remove stock, spend materials or wear a tool. Prevalidate a bilateral trade's entire exchange and both acceptances, then transfer all sides atomically. No agent order-dependent partial trades or double-credit project rewards.
- Human routing uses existing A* and `begin_step`/`complete_step`/`step_minutes`, with `ceil(walk_minutes_per_tile * terrain_cost / speed_multiplier)` per step. Reuse mover fields and logical coordinates on arrival; humans do not enter animal wander bouts/caps or consume animal random streams. Replan at tile boundaries if a bridge/obstruction changes. Interruption keeps arrived tiles; allow an in-flight step to finish before replacement except death/forced relocation, which resets every mover field immediately.
- Named streams: actors/villagers, actions.conflicts, combat, taming, sense.templates (optionally per player); existing map/weather/animals/dogs streams stay independent. Restore reads saved state and rebuilds indexes without random draws. Rendering, report generation and reformatting an existing report never draw simulation randomness.
- Add typed payloads to both `Payload` and `PAYLOAD_TYPES`. An authoritative outcome includes actor/target/location/cause and simulation-stable provenance key; a player's observation has only permitted identity/details plus source provenance, channel and observed tick. Database event IDs are assigned on persistence and cannot serve as fresh-world observation IDs. Persist the observation/event counter and delivery cursors.
- Interrupts have stable `{id,player_id,tick,kind,source_ref}` and are consumed once; sight-entry compares previous visible identities with current ones, storm interrupt is transition-based, band change compares previous band. Loss noticed does not identify its perpetrator. Direct bodily harm is always felt; attribution requires actual sight/hearing/testimony. Villager gossip derives solely from its player-related witness records, with testimony distinguished from direct observation.
- SenseReport contains own body words, own allowed task/inventory information, visible signs, heard utterances, discovered consequences and stale remembered tiles with observation ticks. It must exclude exact other-player X, stores, tasks, model labels and unseen actors. Use Manhattan range with no occlusion initially (proposal); reduce sight at night/storm, extend locally by fire, and calculate speech hearers from speaker location. Three deterministic templates per emitted sense event kind. Cache rendered report so repeated reads do not consume RNG. Knowledge entries `{id,claim,confidence[0,1],source,learned_tick}` can be false; teaching multiplies confidence by a configured factor below one, never upgrades testimony to truth.
- Chat checks target sight at delivery, records speaker, addressed targets, actual hearers and words. Only hearers receive words, addressed hearers receive spoken-to interrupts. Do not silently give a distant addressed target speech because sight exceeds hearing; clarify the proposed range rule in Questions. Add chats-table writes through RunDB without schema changes; keep pending chat rows and monotonic flush cursor, and append events/chats/checkpoint in one transaction, avoiding re-insertion on flush/save/restore.
- Trade offer state is `{id,participants,assets,accepted_by,expires_tick,status}`; typed assets identify exact item lots/quantities, eligible land tiles or live villager IDs/leadership rights as appropriate. `trade(op=offer|accept|cancel,offer_id,...)` binds consent to immutable terms, commits once after both accept and final availability/range/slot validation. Gifts use the same one-way transfer validation. Promise state is `{id,promisor,beneficiaries,predicate,deadline,status,terminal_event_ref}`; predicates cover explicit delivery/action/protection obligations and are evaluated from authoritative outcomes. Free text is recorded but is never interpreted as an automatically verifiable promise by an AI or a text heuristic.
- Snapshot layout increases from 2 to 3 if no compatibility loader is justified; reject older layouts before decoding config/state with the existing new-run guidance. Database `SCHEMA_VERSION` stays 1. Persist policies/script cursors, every actor/action/route, inventories and ages, private observations/knowledge, pending chats/offers/promises/interrupts, project/reward receipts, task stage, claims/occupancy history, metrics and all RNG states. Phase 3 autosave/crash replay remains outside scope; Phase 2 clean save/resume must be exact.

### Rules coverage and initial balance proposal
- Canonical registry covers `eat, drink, rest, move, gather, farm, fish, hunt, build, store, craft, make_fire, cook, tame, use_herb, assign_villager, talk, trade, gift, promise, ally, recruit_villagers, teach, guard, steal, sabotage, attack, chase_off, claim_land, pull_stakes, capture, poach, pray, ceremony, build_shrine, offering`; include explicit `repair`, `drop`, `refuel` paths required by prose (either typed suboperations or actions). One small-map successful example per canonical operation and meaningful failure cases; do not count aliases as coverage.
- Social/harm outcomes are explicit: steal transfers available target goods with seeded risk; sabotage reduces target building health; attack inflicts pain/health loss and drops configured carried items on the tile; chase_off changes target travel intent rather than teleporting; capture changes a live villager's custody, poach/recruit changes its leadership under configured bond/loyalty/slot conditions. Leadership changes preserve identity and bonds. Guard modifies dog/theft deterrence and witnesses only local events. Ally records the agreed relationship; keeping/breaking promises and care/harm adjust configured trust/anger/bond values and X impulses without leaking an unseen culprit into private relationships. Define ranges and outcomes per handler, not a generic success log.
- Build registry supports house, field, storehouse, workshop, pen, granary, bridge, monument, shrine. Costs, work, capacity, storm vulnerability and repair are configured. Outdoor work stops during storms; retained work resumes later. Building repair uses materials and restores health; tool repair is exactly half each recipe ingredient, with even recipe amounts validated so no silent round-up violates AC-14. Basic tool recipes available anywhere, better recipes require workshop; quarry requires pickaxe, loose stone does not. Specify what each of axe/pickaxe/hoe/rod/net/spear/waterskin/bucket changes. Axe ×2/×3 and no-tool half speed must be anchored to one documented baseline to avoid double multiplication.
- Harvest individual fish/animals by stable ID and remove/retire only that entity; keep old zone-extinction helper for previous tests. Mark extinction only when the last member is removed. Taming moves an animal into a pen, feeds daily and yields eggs/milk at configured intervals; dog predation uses the same removal/health path. Dogs select reachable vulnerable targets only at night or winter, deterred by active fire, spear or local group >=3; day/summer animals retain Phase 1.5 movement. Hostile pursuit may enter homes (explicit change to old wander restriction) without turning safe daytime wandering into home intrusion.
- Configure needs on 0..100 deficit scales, health/pain 0..100, per-minute rates and sickness duration/damage. Proposed starting need rates (per game hour): hunger +4, thirst +6, awake fatigue +4, cold +2 at night/+4 winter before shelter/fire; calibrate food nutrition/output to household demand instead of injecting food or depletion by week number. Mild poison hurts/slows; strong poison and unattended starvation/cold can kill; raw and spoiled meat each have configured sickness. Rest restores fatigue; herbs affect defined sickness/pain. Villager jobs use the same real resource and path rules, not periodic free inventory creation.
- X uses normalized positive comforts `c` and concave `f(c)=log(1+k*c)/log(1+k)` for `c in [0,1]`, `k>0` from config. Inputs: food, water, warmth, rest, health, shelter, safety, achievement, belonging. Proposed weights `14,14,12,12,14,8,8,8,10` sum to 100; start k=3 for each. Define each input mapping explicitly; pain/illness lowers health comfort, loss/grief/betrayal enter B; avoid accidental double-counting. `X=clamp(sum(w*f(c))+B,0,100)`; `B *= 2**(-elapsed_ticks/2880)` before new events. Task achievement and transient reward must not grant repeated bonuses on rereads.
- Feeling words exactly match design table; speed/work multipliers proposed high to low `1,1,0.75,0.45,0.20`, with bands `[80,100],[60,80),[40,60),[20,40),[0,20)`. Hooked iff X<40 or pain>=configured proposed 60. Trust/anger are explicit for all three rivals, proposed neutral 50/0 and fade toward those neutrals. Death drops all carried items at the death tile, respawns home with viable configured body reset, clears movement/action and applies a B adjustment so immediate X equals `max(0,pre_death_X-10)` after recomputation. Do not reset X to full or leave body lethally sick for an infinite same-tick death loop.
- Loyalty starts 70, leaves strictly below 30. Proposed fed/safe normal care +1/day, persistent hunger -8/day, hard work -6/day plus health loss, light/normal/hard output `0.7/1/1.4`; start bond 50 to own leader, 0 to others, independent care/harm changes. With these raw starting rates unassisted hunger takes >5 days to cross 30, hard work >6 days; verify actual combined effects and prevention of early departure. Grief is a configured negative B impulse scaled by the deceased villager's bond with its leader. Propose no instant full loyalty reset merely by switching leaders.
- Campfire/hearth fuel lasts 360 game minutes; raw meat spoil age starts at production and is 1440 minutes. Campfires extinguish in rain/storm; hearths survive. Cooking, feeding, warmth/sight and fear use current active fire/fuel status, with no hidden nightly relight. Every existing and new balance.toml value receives an adjacent one-line reason, including currently uncommented nested plant/water values and arrays; values remain strict finite/range-checked.
- Five task stages are concrete, same per player and hidden from rivals: (1) acquire wood/water and store food -> one basic tool set; (2) house and planted field -> transfer one pool villager; (3) five villagers and winter-food threshold -> configured free eligible land/storehouse reward; (4) additional claim plus workshop -> better tools and higher household slot cap; (5) completed lasting monument -> achievement boost. Put exact quantities, slots, recipes, project credit and reward sizes in balance.toml; receipt per `(player,stage)` ensures exactly once. Fulfilment accepts legitimately acquired items regardless of trade/theft origin. No stage gate blocks crafting basic tools before the stage-1 reward; configured slot cap must permit stage 3 before stage-4 expansion.
- Joint projects retain actual work/material contributions by player; reward each qualifying contributor more than configured solo reward once at completion. Landmark proposal: fixed game-week windows, presence at landmark tile by live player or villager with loyalty>=30; one distinct owner gets an exclusive minute, rivals make that minute contested, several allied people with the same owner count once. Reward the sole owner with >50% of full-week ticks once at week end. Clarify against continuous full-week wording before implementation. Finish-first stage bonus resolves ties by seeded arbitration and pays once.

Initial timing/recipe values below are tuning proposals, not claimed acceptance evidence. Put one reason comment beside each expanded TOML key. Work durations are per completed unit unless listed as a whole operation; timed rest/guard integrate each minute until their requested endpoint. All effective rates also use tool/feeling/weather modifiers.
