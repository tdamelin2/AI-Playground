### Idea 1: One movement state machine with bounded start admission
What: Introduce pure movement primitives for starting, completing and exposing a step, with per-entity persisted timing and bout state. One movement job completes due steps, then admits due starts in stable global entity-id order, using animal/dog policy functions for targets and activity. Stagger initial and newborn starts using their named RNG streams; cap starts per kind per tick at floor(population/4) for populations >=4, deferring only unstarted steps by one tick. | Covers: AC-1, AC-2, AC-4, AC-5, AC-6, AC-18.
Why better: Random offsets alone cannot guarantee the strict 25% ceiling. Admission preserves independent clocks while preventing later random collisions; accepted steps still finish exactly on time. Keep pack identity/cohesion but stop moving an entire pack on one coin toss. A generic begin_step(entity, destination, tick, duration)/complete_step(entity, tick) seam supports Phase 2 without prematurely implementing players or routes. | Trade-off: Admission adds small queueing delays and needs a documented population<4 exception; no pure random jitter can satisfy AC-2 universally. | Effort: Medium.

### Idea 2: Commit-on-arrival movement and game-time interpolation
What: Persist source AND destination coordinates, start/end ticks, next_move_tick and remaining bout steps. x/y remain the committed source until completion. WorldFrame copies the whole segment; drawing computes p=clamp((frame.tick+sub_tick-start)/(end-start),0,1), then rounds position in 16-pixel sprite space before multiplying by integer scale. | Covers: AC-5, AC-6, AC-8, AC-19.
Why better: Avoids each tile being visually traversed again every sub-tick cycle, keeps collision/sense positions honest, and avoids blurred fractional screen pixels. Idle frames use from=to=current and zero duration, with an explicit zero-duration branch. Use keyword/default movement fields to preserve current positional Animal/Dog construction; initialize all timing through the same helper after generation and at birth/recolonisation. | Trade-off: AC-8's literal sub_tick endpoints only describe a one-tick segment; multi-tick segments require elapsed tick time as well. | Effort: Medium.
