# Phase 1.5: Colour and time-true movement
Status: Stage: Done | Round: 0/3 | Updated: 2026-11-02 09:53

## For Tim
This phase makes the island look and move the way you hoped. The map turns to colour, the animals get the Tiny Creatures pictures, and every animal and wild dog moves on its own timer at its own speed (a rabbit hops about once a second at speed x1, a goat ambles more slowly). They glide smoothly between squares instead of jumping. Daylight now fades over an hour at dusk and comes back over an hour at dawn. Autumn trees turn orange and yellow, and drought turns the grass yellow. A setting still switches back to the old black-and-white look.
The work is split between two builders. One handles the island's movement rules and saving. The other handles the pictures, colours and the window. They agree in advance on exactly what information passes between them.
Ideas from Codex that we're using: keep the save-file version separate from the database version; cap how many animals of one kind can start moving in the same minute, so they never move in lockstep; draw the light as one layer over the whole screen; and keep the old speeding-up rules for dogs at night and in winter.
One thing to know: old saved games can't be opened after this phase. For your review you'll start a fresh game with a new name.
You are approving the plan below. It costs $0.

Due individuals come from an in-memory (due tick, id) heap rebuilt from saved entity fields on load (nothing extra persisted, no RNG draws on restore).

When bout_remaining reaches 0, rest is drawn (uniform inclusive) and next_move_tick = tick + rest.
  - Flee: at each step decision of a flees_dogs animal, if a dog's logical tile is within flee_radius, the animal steps to the valid neighbour farthest from the nearest dog at flee_minutes_per_tile; flee steps don't use bout tiles and are followed by another decision at their end. Resting animals don't check for dogs (Phase 2 can add an interrupt).
  - Dogs: each dog has its own timer; a dog farther than cohesion from its pack leader steps toward the leader, otherwise it wanders.
