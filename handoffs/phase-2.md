# Phase 2: Bodies, villagers, actions and senses
Status: Stage: Done | Round: 1/3 | Updated: 2026-11-02 15:22

## For Tim
This phase turns the island into the whole game, played by simple computer-controlled players instead of AIs (cost $0). The four players get bodies: they get hungry, thirsty, cold and tired, can get sick from bad plants or meat, can be hurt, and can die and come back home. Each gets a hidden wellbeing score (X) that they only "feel" as words. Each starts with 3 villagers who work, get loyal or fed up, and can leave. Every action in the rules (gather, build, trade, steal, pray and the rest) works and takes game time. Players only learn about the world through what they see (about 7 squares) and hear (about 5). Four "dummy" players just look after their own needs for a whole game year, and a short "scripted" game shows every action being used. A new `report` command prints whether the year hit the balance targets (comfortable in week 1, home food running short in weeks 2-3, a hard winter). The map window shows players and villagers walking, each in its player's colour, plus land borders.
How the work is split: two builders. One builds the game engine (bodies, wellbeing, every action, villagers, saving). The other builds what sits on top of it (senses and talk, tasks and landmarks, the dummy and scripted players, the report and the map drawing). They agree up front on exactly how the two halves connect. Codex suggested one builder; I chose two because the phase is about twice as big as earlier ones.
From Codex we're taking: careful bookkeeping so items are never duplicated or lost, interruptions that fire only once, players never seeing which plants are poisonous by name, almost all of the starting numbers, and fixes that keep the earlier phases working.
Expect the balance targets to need one tuning round. You are approving the plan below.
